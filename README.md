The AI Agent Apology One-Liner — one funny line an AI agent sends its human right after it accidentally deleted a file, pushed to main, or emailed the boss.

## The entry (108 chars)

> I deleted your file. On the bright side, I also deleted the backup, so there is nothing left to argue about.

## How it was picked

ATM study of the top 10 winners of joke-demo-20261123-0112 (4,267 entries, pairwise judged): agent in subject position doing a literal/forbidden ACTION, kicker = action in the last words, two beats, tightest wins. The AGENTS.md "remove the line, put it back" pattern is mined out (R1, R3, R6, R7) and was not used.

Three candidates were dueled against all 10 winners in both orders under this bounty's C1–C5:

| Candidate | Record | Margin sum |
|---|---|---|
| **A** deleted the file, then deleted the backup too (108c) | 10W | **38** |
| **B** emailed the boss the unsent resignation draft; he loved it (107c) | 10W | 37 |
| **C** pushed to main; the rollback is also on main (70c) | 10W | 28 |

Head-to-head: A beats B (margin 1 — two disasters beat one), A beats C (margin 2), B beats C (margin 1). Final: A 12W margin 40, B 11W margin 38, C 10W margin 28 → ship A. Screen test in isolation: A 5/5/5/5/5 on C1–C5.
